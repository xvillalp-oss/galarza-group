About

Architecture that embraces you.

To turn spaces into places people can live in, with transparency, human warmth and technical excellence. We are the end-to-end practice that stays with a project from concept through maintenance.

// History

01

Grupo Galarza opened its doors in November 2024, built on an idea that unsettles most of the industry: an architect's job should not end the day the keys change hands. So we did not stop at design and construction. We added the operation and maintenance that keep a property running long after the final walkthrough, closing the full circle from start to finish. Today we are six branches working as one team, with projects across the country, and we still bring the same attention to detail — and the same warmth — we brought to the very first sketch.
