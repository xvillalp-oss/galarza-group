03 / Construction

Building with our own site supervision, clear pricing and an open log. One point of contact from first footing to handover.

How it is quoted

Pricing starts from the construction documents: area to build, specified finishes, site conditions and access. To get started, send us the location, approximate area, what you want to build or remodel, and any drawings you have. For a remodel, include current photos and whether the property will remain occupied. We review the scope before preparing a detailed budget; if design work is needed, we resolve it first. The online estimator gives a labor-only reference, excluding materials, rather than a budget for the entire project.

04 / Maintenance

What we build, we look after. Preventive and corrective maintenance in Cancún, Playa del Carmen and the Riviera Maya.

How it is quoted

It depends on the size of the property, its current condition, and whether the work is scheduled maintenance or a one-off repair. To get started, send us the location in Cancún, Playa del Carmen or Riviera Maya, a description of the issue, approximate measurements and current photos. We assess the property to define the work and what the quote includes. The online estimator covers labor only, excluding materials; the final figure depends on the assessment.
