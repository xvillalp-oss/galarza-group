04 // Services

Maintenance

What we build, we look after. Preventive and corrective maintenance in Cancún, Playa del Carmen and the Riviera Maya.

// What we do

01

That care extends to buildings someone else put up. We handle preventive and corrective maintenance so a property does not deteriorate between projects: scheduled check-ups, targeted repairs, and a team that in many cases already knows the building because it built it. We work with anything from a single home to a full development, with the same in-house supervision and open log we use on construction sites.

What it includes

  • Scheduled, periodic inspections of the property

  • Preventive maintenance for systems and finishes

  • Corrective repairs when something breaks

  • An open log for every visit and repair

  • Coordination with our other branches when the job calls for it

Who it is for

Owners already living in their home, developers managing a project, or businesses renting out property who do not want wear and tear to pile up. It also fits clients who built with us and want the same team to keep caring for it, and owners who built elsewhere but are now looking for someone to trust with the upkeep.

How it is quoted

It depends on the size of the property, its current condition, and whether the work is scheduled maintenance or a one-off repair. To get started, send us the location in Cancún, Playa del Carmen or Riviera Maya, a description of the issue, approximate measurements and current photos. We assess the property to define the work and what the quote includes. The online estimator covers labor only, excluding materials; the final figure depends on the assessment.

// How this branch works

02

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We walk the property and note what needs attention now and what can be scheduled.

  2. 02

    Maintenance plan

    We rank the tasks by priority and frequency, preventive and corrective.

  3. 03

    Execution

    The same team that supervises new construction handles the work, with the log open.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    We check the repair holds and adjust the plan as things develop.
